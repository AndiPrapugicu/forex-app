# Australian dollar (AUD)

## Central bank: the Reserve Bank of Australia
- **Mandate.** Price stability, with an **inflation target of 2–3%**, and full employment. (RBA, about monetary policy)
- **Who decides.** The **Monetary Policy Board**, independent of government. Its common understanding with the Treasurer is set out in the Statement on the Conduct of Monetary Policy, last published in July 2025. (RBA)
- **Instrument.** The **cash rate target**. (RBA)
- **Meetings.** **Eight a year**, timed after key data releases. (RBA)
- **Gap in our feeds:** the RBA's media-release feed refuses automated reads, so the dossier carries no RBA statement text. Use the calendar and headlines, and say that the bank's own words were not available.

## Economy: a resources exporter tied to China
- In **2024–25** Australia's top exports were **iron ore and concentrates (A$116.4bn)**, **coal (A$71.3bn)** and **natural gas (A$64.7bn)**. (DFAT, goods and services exports 2024–25)
- **China** took **A$212.4bn**, or **29.2% of total exports**, making it by far the largest market. (DFAT)
  - *Implication (background):* the AUD trades as a proxy for Chinese demand and global industrial activity. Iron ore is not in our cross-asset feed; **copper** is used as the nearest available proxy, and should be named as a proxy.

## Channels that move the Aussie (background)
1. **Commodity prices and China**: iron ore, coal, LNG and Chinese activity data.
2. **Risk appetite.** The AUD is pro-cyclical: it tends to fall in risk-off episodes and rise with equities.
3. **The RBA vs Fed path**, and the 2-year gap.
4. **Domestic inflation and the labour market**: the monthly CPI indicator and employment change.

## How to read AUD in a pair
AUD is the base of AUDUSD, AUDJPY, AUDCAD, AUDCHF and AUDNZD, and the quote of EURAUD and GBPAUD.
