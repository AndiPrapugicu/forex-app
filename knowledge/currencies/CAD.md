# Canadian dollar (CAD)

## Central bank: the Bank of Canada
- **Mandate.** Inflation control with a **2% target, the midpoint of a 1–3% range**. It is agreed jointly with the federal government and renewed every five years; the current agreement runs to **31 December 2026**. (BoC, monetary policy)
- **Instrument.** The **target for the overnight rate** (the policy rate). (BoC)
- **Meetings.** **Eight fixed announcement dates a year**, each followed by a press conference. The Monetary Policy Report is published four times a year. (BoC)
- **Preferred core measures:** CPI-trim and CPI-median, not headline CPI alone. (BoC)

## Economy: oil, sold to the US
- In 2025 Canada exported **4.3 million barrels a day of crude, 90.1% of it to the US**. Crude exports were worth **C$140.0bn, of which C$126.1bn went to the US**. (Canada Energy Regulator, 2025 Canada–US energy trade)
- Energy exports to the US (crude, refined products, natural gas and NGLs) were **20.2% of Canada's total goods exports** in 2025. (CER)
- Crude oil has been Canada's **largest export commodity every year since 2008**, except 2015, 2016 and 2018. (CER, via StatCan data)
  - *Implication (background):* WTI and Canadian heavy-crude prices feed the terms of trade. US trade policy is a first-order risk, because the US is the destination for almost all of these exports.

## Channels that move the loonie (background)
1. **Oil prices** (WTI in the cross-asset section).
2. **The BoC vs Fed path.** The two economies are tightly linked, so the spread often matters more than the levels.
3. **US–Canada trade policy**: tariffs and counter-measures.
4. **Risk appetite**, to a lesser degree than the AUD and NZD.

## How to read CAD in a pair
CAD is the quote of USDCAD, EURCAD, GBPCAD, AUDCAD and NZDCAD, so **CAD strength is bearish for them**, and the base of CADJPY and CADCHF.
