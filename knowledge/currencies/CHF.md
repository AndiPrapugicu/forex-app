# Swiss franc (CHF)

## Central bank: the Swiss National Bank
- **Mandate.** Price stability, defined as **CPI inflation below 2% a year**, while taking account of economic developments. (SNB, monetary policy)
- **Instruments.** The **SNB policy rate**, and **foreign exchange interventions when necessary**. (SNB)
- **Meetings.** A monetary policy assessment **once a quarter**: March, June, September and December. Between assessments the SNB acts mostly through the FX market, if at all. (SNB)

## A safe-haven currency
- The SNB describes the franc as a **safe haven**. Global stress and geopolitical tension create **appreciation pressure**, which tightens Swiss monetary conditions and depresses imported inflation. The SNB has said its willingness to intervene stays high to counter **rapid and excessive appreciation**. (SNB speech, 22 November 2024)
- Switzerland has run a **current account surplus since the 1980s**, around 10% of GDP in 2016. The SNB attributes it to structural factors (a large net foreign asset position, the financial centre, merchanting), not to an undervalued franc. (SNB speech, 23 November 2017)

## A dated episode: the floor that broke
- On **15 January 2015** the SNB dropped its **CHF 1.20 per euro minimum exchange rate**, which had become unsustainable without interventions of rapidly growing size. At the same time it cut the sight-deposit rate to **−0.75%**. The move surprised markets, and EURCHF briefly fell to around **0.85**. (SNB, "our.snb.ch" minimum exchange rate; SNB speech, April 2015)
  - *Lesson (background):* an FX policy commitment can end without warning when defending it becomes too costly. Positioning against the SNB is a tail risk.

## Channels that move the franc (background)
1. **Risk sentiment and geopolitics**: the haven bid.
2. **The SNB's stance on the franc**: intervention, or tolerance of strength when inflation is a concern.
3. **Rate gap to the ECB.** The policy rate is near zero, so the franc is a low yielder, and calm carry markets tend to weaken it.
4. **Euro-area stress**: EURCHF falls when euro sovereign risk rises.

## How to read CHF in a pair
CHF is the quote of USDCHF, EURCHF, GBPCHF and the other franc crosses, so **franc strength is bearish for them**.
