# New Zealand dollar (NZD)

## Central bank: the Reserve Bank of New Zealand
- **Remit.** The Minister of Finance issues a remit to the **Monetary Policy Committee**: keep future annual CPI inflation **between 1% and 3% over the medium term, with a focus on the 2% midpoint**. The Reserve Bank of New Zealand (Economic Objective) Amendment Act 2023 refocused the mandate on **price stability** and kept that target. (NZ legislation, MPC remit; NZ Treasury)
- **Instrument.** The **Official Cash Rate (OCR)**.
- **Gap in our feeds:** the RBNZ's feed lists its OCR decisions, but its pages refuse automated reads. The dossier therefore carries the decision headline ("OCR increased by 25 basis points to …") without the statement text. Say so when it matters.

## Economy: dairy and China
- **Milk powder, butter and cheese** were **29% of New Zealand's goods exports (NZ$21.8bn)** in the year ended March 2025, the largest commodity group. In the year ended December 2025 they led the rise in exports, up NZ$4.2bn to NZ$24.3bn. (Stats NZ)
- The top export destinations are **China (25%)**, the US (13%) and Australia (12%). (Stats NZ)
  - *Implication (background):* dairy prices (the Global Dairy Trade auctions, not in our feeds) and Chinese demand drive the terms of trade. **Copper** in the cross-asset section is only a loose proxy for Asian demand.

## Channels that move the kiwi (background)
1. **RBNZ vs Fed and RBA path.** The RBNZ has historically moved early and in large steps.
2. **Dairy prices and China.**
3. **Risk appetite**: the NZD is pro-cyclical, like the AUD.
4. **AUDNZD** mostly reflects the RBA–RBNZ policy gap and relative terms of trade.

## How to read NZD in a pair
NZD is the base of NZDUSD, NZDJPY, NZDCAD and NZDCHF, and the quote of AUDNZD, EURNZD and GBPNZD.
